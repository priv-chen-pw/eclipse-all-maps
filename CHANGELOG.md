# Changelog

## 2.0.0

* **Final stage:** the stage 5 Primordial Teleporter, aligned with the moon, leads to a random final stage: Commencement (Mithrix), the Planetarium (Voidling), Prime Meridian (False Son), Solutional Haunt (Solus Wing) or Soulbound Threshold (Anima Harvester). Chat drops a cryptic hint about which one, and none comes twice until the run has had them all. New settings section: Final Stage, with a checkbox per final stage.
* **After the final boss:** every final stage has a way to end the run with a win and one on to the next loop. Commencement and the Planetarium get a Destination Portal to stage 1; Solutional Haunt, and in Eclipse Prime Meridian and Soulbound Threshold, get a Celestial Portal that ends the run. The added portal stands next to the map's own exit. New setting: End or loop after the final boss.
* **Soulbound Threshold:** as the final stage its monsters have 60% health and the Anima Harvester 80%. New setting: Weaker Soulbound Threshold.
* **Shrine of Descent:** after its 666% charge the teleporter works as usual and no Ritual Portal opens. New setting: Shrine of Descent keeps the teleporter.
* **Frog:** no longer opens portals to Prime Meridian and Neural Sanctum, and both settings are gone. In Eclipse those routes never offered their ending anyway: Eclipse hides the Shrine of Rebirth and refuses Solus Heart's offering, so the 1.1.0 README was wrong about that.
* **Void Locus:** its teleporter boss is a Void Devastator, Imp Overlord, Xi Construct, Wandering Vagrant or Grandparent. Before, its pool gave mostly Void Devastators, more a big monster than a boss. New setting: Void bosses.
* **Void Locus:** leaving it begins a loop, as leaving any stage 5 does. Before, a loop from Void Locus didn't count: after it Golden Dieback didn't replace Treeborn Colony, and access codes no longer opened their portal to Conduit Canyon on stage 3.
* **README:** shorter and simpler, with a note that the mod is made only for more randomized Eclipse runs. The settings list is gone: the settings explain themselves in game.

## 1.1.0

* **Conduit Canyon:** its teleporter boss is a Solus Control Unit, Alloy Worship Unit, Solus Amalgamator or Alloy Hunter, equally likely. New setting: Robot bosses.
* **Conduit Canyon:** its boss fight no longer gets 800 extra credits from the map. Since 1.0.2 those credits bought extra bosses.
* **Alloy Hunter:** it comes alone, plus one more per Shrine of the Mountain, each with the health of the extra hunters the boss credits would have bought. Before, late in a run it came four or five at a time (example: stage 5, 35 minutes, one Mountain shrine). The Alloy Worship Unit works the same way.
* **Alloy Hunter:** it is no longer certain on stages without bosses of their own (Void Locus's rare Lunar family event), and it isn't added under the Artifact of Kin.
* **Green portals:** none from Gilded Coast or the Bazaar's geodes either (part of No green portals).
* **Solutional Haunt portals:** solved access codes and Siren's Call no longer open a portal to Solutional Haunt; the access codes' extra boss and item stay. New setting: No Solutional Haunt portals.
* **Frog:** after Mithrix, it also opens a portal to Neural Sanctum (Solus Heart); accepting Solus Heart's offering is a win. New setting: Frog portal to Neural Sanctum.
* **Halcyon Shrine:** from stage 4, its monster waves get half the credits and its Halcyonite half the bonus from the gold. New setting: Weaker Halcyon Shrines from stage 4.
* **README:** shorter, with an early-version note.

## 1.0.2

* Halcyon Shrines can spawn on every stage like any other interactable, with their usual cost and odds, and no longer open a green portal. New setting: Halcyon Shrine on every stage.
* One setting, Alloy Hunter as a normal boss, now covers both the Alloy Hunter in stage 4+ boss pools and the single boss on Conduit Canyon. Off, the Alloy Hunter is Conduit Canyon's second boss again, as in vanilla. It replaces the Conduit Canyon "One boss" setting.
* The README lists each added map's changes and shows the Halcyon Shrine, the frog and the Alloy Hunter.

## 1.0.0

First release.

* Reformed Altar (stage 2), Treeborn Colony (stage 3), Conduit Canyon (stage 4) and Void Locus (stage 5) join the stage rotation of Eclipse runs.
* They are balanced for their stage: each gets its stage's median chest and monster credits. Void Locus gets a Primordial Teleporter to Commencement instead of Deep Void Signals, a stage 5's interactables, and a stage 5 monster mix on the first loop. Conduit Canyon's teleporter leads to stage 5, with one boss.
* Reformed Altar and Treeborn Colony open no green Colossus portal. After Mithrix, petting Commencement's frog 10 times opens one to Prime Meridian.
* The Alloy Hunter can be the teleporter boss on any map from stage 4 on.
* Settings for everything in Mod Options: a checkbox per map grouped by stage, a switch per change, a slider for how often the added maps roll, and an option to use the mod in normal runs too.
